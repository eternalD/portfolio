### Project overview
There'd been a couple of earlier attempts at personas before this project started. The good news: the old material was actually solid — it just never made it past the desk drawer. My job was to figure out how to get it into daily use. 

### My Role
I ran the research — remote interviews across different user groups — then distilled it all into personas the team could actually use day to day.

### Design Process
I interviewed 12 participants across different user groups and synthesized the findings into four persona archetypes, giving the team a clearer, shared view of the people they were designing for.

### Key Learnings
The strongest driver of adoption wasn't the persona document itself—it was involving stakeholders early and validating the archetypes before rollout.

### Business outcomes
The team finally had personas they actually used — a shared, grounded reference point that stuck because it was built with them, not handed to them.

### Team
A mixed team: Me, key stakeholders from their account team.
