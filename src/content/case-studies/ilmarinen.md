### Project overview
The redesign targeted WCAG AA conformance across the website, treating accessibility as a foundation of the experience rather than a later addition.

### My Role & Tasks
I coordinated the accessibility audit across thousands of redesigned pages, distributed the review work among several designers, and automated repeatable checks with accessibility tools. I then consolidated the findings into a consistent set of recommendations.

### Design Process
We began by speaking with users and gathering research to define the core problems. The team then explored and tested multiple concepts before refining the chosen direction. As the redesign progressed, audit work was distributed across designers, repeatable accessibility checks were automated where possible, and findings were consolidated before handoff.

### Key Learnings
Accessibility at this scale required shared ownership, consistent audit criteria, and repeatable tooling. Treating it as an ongoing design and development practice made the review manageable.

### Business outcomes
The audit created a consistent accessibility baseline across thousands of pages and gave the team a prioritized path for remediation.

### Team
The project team included service, UX, and visual designers; developers and architects; and client-side project leads.
