Kemppi develops welding equipment, services, and management technology. The company was responding to a growing skills gap as experienced welders retired and demand for qualified workers increased. WeldEye connected Kemppi equipment to a cloud-based welding management solution, helping teams capture weld data and support inspections.

The product used a serverless AWS architecture, which was new to Kemppi at the time. Nordcloud joined as the implementation partner, bringing experience with AWS services and serverless development.

### My Role
I facilitated Lean UX workshops and co-design sessions, led a design sprint, and collaborated with Kemppi and its external technology partner on the interface. I also contributed to the front-end implementation using HTML, CSS, and AngularJS.

### Design Process
Lean UX cycles and coded prototypes allowed the team to test workflow assumptions and technical constraints early, then refine the product through short feedback loops.

### Key Learnings
Coded prototypes exposed workflow and technical constraints earlier than static mockups. Shared goals also helped Kemppi, its external technology partner, and Nordcloud make decisions together.

### Business outcomes
The resulting WeldEye experience gave welders and inspectors a shared digital workflow for accessing equipment data and supporting inspections.

### Team
The project team included Nordcloud designers, developers, and architects; designers and developers from the external technology partner; and Kemppi project stakeholders.
